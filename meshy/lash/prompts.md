# Lash: Meshy prompts

Replace `{{CONCEPT}}` with a description of your character, for example *Rook, a hulking stone golem with glowing blue runes and a lantern for a heart*, and `{{CHARACTER}}` with its short name (*Rook*). Or set `concept` and `select.name` in `workspace.json` and run `dlmod meshy lash` again to fill them in.

Everything: about 206 credits (156 without the optional steps). Prices are Meshy's as of 2026-10; confirm before generating.

## 1. Character design sheet

- Tool: `meshy_text_to_image`, model `nano-banana-pro`, about 9 credits
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Save as: `assets/design_sheet.png`
- Every later picture uses this sheet as its reference, so redo it until the character is right.

```text
Character design sheet for a 3D game character: {{CONCEPT}}. It replaces Lash in Deadlock, a stylized third-person hero shooter (Lash: Dives down to stomp his foes). Its abilities are Ground Strike, Grapple, Flog, Death Slam. The character must read at a glance from behind in third person, so give it a strong silhouette and one or two bold colours. It carries its own take on Lash's weapon, Tale of The Tape. Show a large hero render, a front / three-quarter / side / back turnaround in a relaxed A-pose, close-ups of the head, hands and weapon, a colour palette with hex codes, and its two or three iconic symbols. Stylized PBR, clean studio light, dark background, labels only.
```

## 2. Front, side and back views for 3D

```text

```

## 3. 3D model

- Tool: `meshy_multi_image_to_3d`, model `meshy-7.1`, texture `meshy-7`, about 30 credits
- Reference: `assets/views.png`
- Options: `enable_pbr: false`, `texture_resolution: 2k`
- Save as: `assets/model.glb`, replaces `models/heroes_wip/lash/lash.vmdl`
- Detailed geometry reads best close up in third person, and Meshy models of about 200k triangles install fine. dlmod scales it to Lash's height (105 units) and rigs it to Lash's skeleton. For a lighter model use meshy_image_to_3d with meshy-t2.5 + meshy-n1 (15 credits, target_polycount 60000) on the front view.

## 4. Rig (humanoid characters) (optional)

- Tool: `meshy_rig`, about 5 credits
- Reference: `assets/model.glb`
- Save as: `assets/model.glb`
- Only for characters with arms and legs: dlmod maps Meshy's joints to the hero's bones by their structure. Skip it for blob-like characters: dlmod rigs them on the hero's own bones (see Animations in README.md). Meshy's animation clips are not used: the game plays the hero's own clips.

## 5. Signature weapon or held prop (optional)

- Tool: `meshy_text_to_3d`, model `meshy-t2.5`, about 15 credits
- Options: `refine: true`, `target_polycount: 15000`
- Save as: `assets/weapon.glb`
- Join it to model.glb in the right hand before installing, or keep the hero's own gun with -keep gun.

```text
A single game-ready prop: the signature weapon of {{CONCEPT}}. It is its own take on Lash's weapon, Tale of The Tape. Stylized PBR in the character's colours, centered, no hands, no base.
```
