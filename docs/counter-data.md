# Counter item match data

Measured from deadlock-api match data, 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-19 (since the Minor Update - 11-04-2026 patch), ranked and unranked matches at all ranks. Values are the change in the buyer's win rate, in percentage points, when the hero is on the enemy team. They are corrected for the hero's overall strength and for the item's usual win rate, and compared with other items of the same price. **0.00** means the change was not statistically significant; **n/a** means fewer than 20,000 purchases against that hero.

Read negative values with care. Defensive counters are often bought by players who are already losing to that hero, which pulls their numbers down. A negative value means the match data does not back the pick, not that the item never works.

Generated by `tools/update_counter_stats.py`.

Of the 220 reference picks, 70 raise the buyer's win rate measurably, 92 show no significant change, 58 lower it, and 0 lack data.
